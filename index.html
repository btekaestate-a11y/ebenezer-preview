First time? Perfect.

How to eat with your hands like you've done it forever

No forks, no rules you can't learn in one bite. First-timers walk out converts. Here's the whole ritual:

፩

Tear the injera

The spongy sour flatbread under everything is your plate and your fork. Tear a palm-size piece from the edge.

፪

Scoop, don't stab

Pinch a stew with the injera — lentils, tibs, collards. One confident scoop. The bread does the work.

፫

Share the platter

Everyone eats from the same big plate. It's not a quirk — it's the point. Order one platter for two people.

፬

Finish with buna

Ethiopia is where coffee was born. End with a small, strong cup — the traditional close to a shared meal.
